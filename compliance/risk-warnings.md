# Risk warnings

**Status: normative within the fiction. Illustrative, not verified.**

Required warnings and prohibited claims. A warning is clear, prominent, and never buried.

## Prohibited claims (always)

Never write, for any product:

- "Guaranteed returns", "risk-free", "no risk", "can't lose".
- "Beat the market", "guaranteed approval".
- Anything implying deposits or investments cannot fall in value when they can.

## Deposits and savings

- Everyday deposits are protected up to 100.000 € by the Deposit Guarantee Scheme (see
  `disclosures.md`). Do not imply returns or growth that a current account or Space does
  not provide.

## Credit and overdraft (conditional, CCD2)

If Vanker offers credit or an overdraft, show a clear warning:

- Do: "Borrowing costs money. If you miss payments, you may pay more and it can affect
  your ability to borrow in future."
- State the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) and the total amount payable near the warning.

## Crypto-assets (conditional, MiCA)

If Vanker offers crypto-assets, show a prominent warning before purchase:

- Do: "Crypto-assets are highly volatile. They are not covered by the Deposit Guarantee
  Scheme, and you could lose all the money you put in."
- Provide the required crypto-asset information; no promotion without the warning.

## Investments (conditional, MiFID II)

If Vanker offers investments:

- Do: "Your capital is at risk. The value of investments can go down as well as up, and
  you may get back less than you put in. Past performance does not predict future
  results."
- Run the appropriateness or suitability check before the person invests.

## Placement

- The warning sits with the decision (near the action or the product claim), at a size
  and contrast that make it readable (see `accessibility.md`), never hidden in a footer
  or behind a link alone.

## Eval hooks

- No prohibited-claim terms anywhere.
- Crypto copy (if present) contains the volatility and "not covered by the Deposit
  Guarantee Scheme" warning.
- Investment copy (if present) contains a capital-at-risk warning.
- Credit copy (if present) contains a borrowing-costs warning plus the APR.
