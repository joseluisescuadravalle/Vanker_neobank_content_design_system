# Data and privacy

**Status: normative within the fiction. Illustrative, not verified.**

How Vanker talks about personal data (GDPR) and tracking (ePrivacy). Plain, honest, and
in the person's control.

## Privacy notice (GDPR)

- Explain, in plain language: what data is collected, why, the lawful basis, how long it
  is kept, who it is shared with, and the person's rights.
- Layer it: a short, clear summary up front, with the full notice one tap away.

## Consent

- Where consent is the basis, it is **freely given, specific, and informed**, separate
  from accepting the terms, with **no pre-ticked boxes**.
- Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
- Do: "Can we use your activity to suggest features? You can change this any time." (with
  a real choice, unticked)
- Not: bundling marketing consent into "I accept the terms".

## Tracking and cookies (ePrivacy)

- Ask before non-essential tracking. **Reject must be as easy as accept** (an equally
  prominent "Reject" alongside "Accept").

## Data rights

- Make it easy to access, correct, delete, port, or object to the use of data, and say
  how. Respond within the legal time limit.

## Data minimization

- Collect only what is needed, and never display more personal data on screen than the
  task requires.

## Eval hooks

- Consent requests are unbundled and unticked, with a real choice.
- A cookie or tracking prompt offers "Reject" as prominently as "Accept".
- Privacy copy states purpose, retention, and rights in plain language.
