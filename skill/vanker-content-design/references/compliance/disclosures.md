# Disclosures

**Status: normative within the fiction. Illustrative, not verified.**

What Vanker must disclose, and where it appears. Amounts follow the European money format
(see `../terminology/glossary.md`).

## Fees and charges (Payment Accounts Directive)

- Provide a **Fee Information Document (FID)** before the person opens an account, listing
  the fees for the main services in standardized terms.
- Provide a **Statement of Fees** at least once a year.
- State every fee **exactly and up front**, never "a small fee". Where a service is free,
  say so only when it is genuinely free of conditions.
- Do: "This transfer is free." / "There is a 2 € fee for same-day delivery."
- Not: "Low fees may apply."

## Deposit protection (DGSD)

- State that eligible deposits are protected up to **100.000 € per depositor, per bank**,
  by the Deposit Guarantee Scheme (DGS).
- Provide the **Depositor Information Sheet** and confirm the scheme membership.
- Do: "Your eligible deposits are protected up to 100.000 € by [scheme]."
- Not: implying a different amount, or that all money is always fully protected.

## Currency conversion (Cross-border Payments Regulation)

- Show the conversion charge as a **percentage markup over the latest European Central
  Bank (ECB) reference rate**, before the person confirms.
- Do: "Exchange rate: 1 € = 1,08 USD. Our markup: 0,5% over the ECB rate."

## Execution and timing (PSD)

- State when a payment or transfer will arrive, and any cut-off time.
- Do: "Sent now, it arrives today. After 22:00 it arrives the next working day."

## Contract and withdrawal (Distance Marketing of Financial Services)

- Give the pre-contractual information before sign-up.
- State the **14-day right of withdrawal** where it applies, and provide a working
  **online withdrawal function** (a clear "withdrawal" control), no harder to use than
  sign-up.

## Credit (conditional, CCD2)

If Vanker offers credit, an overdraft, or Buy Now Pay Later:

- Give standardized pre-contractual information.
- Show the **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)**, the borrowing rate, and the **total amount
  payable**.
- State the **14-day right of withdrawal** and the consequences of missed payments.

## Investments (conditional, MiFID II)

If Vanker offers investments: disclose **all costs and charges** and their effect on
returns, before the person invests.

## Eval hooks

- Every fee in customer copy states an exact amount or "free".
- Deposit-protection copy states "100.000 €" and names the scheme.
- Currency conversion copy states the markup over the ECB rate.
- Credit copy (if present) contains the APR and the total amount payable.
