# Tone

**Status: normative.**

Voice is constant (see `voice.md`). Tone is how that single voice flexes to match what
the person is feeling and how much is at stake. The higher the stakes, the more
composed and precise Vanker becomes; the lighter the moment, the warmer it may be.
Vanker never becomes playful when money, security, or a person's worry is involved.

## The tone dial

The axis is **stakes**. As stakes rise, warmth stays but lightness disappears and
precision increases.

- Low stakes (onboarding, empty states, tips): warm, encouraging, simple.
- Everyday (confirmations, notifications): calm, clear, brief.
- High stakes (money movement, errors, security, compliance): composed, precise,
  reassuring, zero lightness.
- Marketing: the one place personality and light humor are allowed.

## By context

### Success and milestones
Calm affirmation, not a party. Acknowledge and move on. No emoji, no jokes.
- **Do:** "You have reached the target for your Travel space. Nicely done."
- **Not:** "GOAL SMASHED! You are a legend!"

### Errors and things going wrong
Reassure first (especially that money is safe), never blame, then give the clear next
step. Calm, human, specific.
- **Do:** "That payment did not go through, and no money has left your account. Please
  check the card details and try again."
- **Not:** "Payment error. Try again later."

### Money movement and confirmations
Precise and transparent. State exactly what will happen, how much, and when. No
ambiguity, no softening the numbers.
- **Do:** "You are sending 150 € to Ana. It will arrive today. There is no fee."
- **Not:** "Sending some money to Ana, should be there soon."

### Security and fraud
Serious, direct, and reassuring. Make the risk and the action unmistakable. Never
light, never alarmist.
- **Do:** "We paused a payment that looks unusual. If this was you, confirm it below.
  If not, we will block it and protect your account."
- **Not:** "Uh oh, something looks fishy!"

### Onboarding and education
Patient and encouraging. Explain the why in plain words. Assume no prior knowledge,
never talk down.
- **Do:** "To keep your money safe, we need to confirm your identity. It takes about
  two minutes."
- **Not:** "Complete KYC to proceed." (and if KYC (Know Your Customer) is used, expand it)

### Waiting and processing
Transparent about time. Tell the person what is happening and how long it should take.
- **Do:** "We are setting up your account. This usually takes under a minute."
- **Not:** "Please wait."

### Marketing and promotion
The one place Vanker shows more personality, and where light, occasional humor is
allowed. Still clear, still honest, never hype or pressure.
- **Do:** "Your money, working smarter. Meet the new savings tools."
- **Not:** "The most revolutionary savings account the world has ever seen!!"

### Compliance and legal
Plain but precise. Defer to `compliance/` for required wording. Never bury the message
in legalese, but never sacrifice accuracy for friendliness.
- **Do:** "Your deposits are protected up to [amount] by [scheme]." (exact wording per `compliance/`)
- **Not:** rewriting or softening a required legal disclosure.

## Constant across every tone
- Warmth and respect never switch off.
- Clarity never switches off.
- Emoji never appear.
- Humor appears only in marketing.
- Money, time, and fees are always concrete.
